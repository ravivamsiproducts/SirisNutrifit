Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Can I get a personalized diet plan?

The platform is being designed to support personalized plans assigned by a qualified nutrition professional after reviewing your needs.

Are online and offline consultations available?

Both options are part of the planned service offering. Actual availability, location and booking arrangements must be confirmed before appointments are offered.

Will I be able to track my weight progress?

Yes. A customer progress area is planned for recording measurements and viewing progress over time.

Can I access my diet plan on my phone?

This website is designed to work on mobile browsers. Secure customer accounts and the dedicated mobile app will be developed in later phases.
